# Shaolin Xu 徐少林

Bioinformatician and population geneticist working on zooplankton (*Daphnia*) and fish.

- **Now:** [Federal Institute of Hydrology (BfG)](https://www.bafg.de/), Koblenz, Germany. Project **FraGen**: developing and validating a non-invasive eDNA-based framework to assess the genetic diversity and population fragmentation of key fish species in German federal waterways, and using it to evaluate fishways and support waterway management.
- **Before:** postdoc at the Leibniz Institute of Freshwater Ecology and Inland Fisheries (IGB), Berlin, on the genomic basis of thermal adaptation in *Daphnia cucullata*; PhD in Ecology at Jinan University, on *Daphnia* of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau.
- **Interests:** environmental DNA, fish population genetics and fragmentation, *Daphnia* population genomics, thermal adaptation, bioinformatics pipelines.
