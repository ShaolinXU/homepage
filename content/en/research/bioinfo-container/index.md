A Docker image for reproducible bioinformatic analysis. It is built on `r-base` and uses [pak](https://pak.r-lib.org/) to install the R packages together with their system dependencies, including `ape`, `vcfR`, `snpR`, `ggtree` and `plotly`.

Source: [ShaolinXU/bioinfo_container](https://github.com/ShaolinXU/bioinfo_container).
