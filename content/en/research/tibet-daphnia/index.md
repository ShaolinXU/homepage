PhD work at Jinan University (promoter: Prof. Dr. Bo-Ping Han).

- Led a field campaign in Tibet to sample *Daphnia tibetana* populations (2018–2021).
- Whole-genome resequencing of *D. tibetana*: spatial pattern of genetic diversity, population demography, and response to a salinity gradient.
- Population genetics and phylogeography of *D. magna* from Tibet and the surrounding area (2016–2018).
